{# What counts as a good answer, and whether the check does its own TLS. The TLS box is not the backend's - they are different layers, and the question "does the check follow the pool" has a footnote. HAProxy sends checks in SSL automatically for a pool reached over SSL, but only while the check goes to the traffic port; a check port breaks the inheritance, which is what the generator writes `check-ssl` for. This box is the other case: a plain pool whose health endpoint speaks TLS, or a tcp pool to be checked by handshake.
